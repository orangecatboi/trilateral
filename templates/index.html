Our Story

Built from Site Experience, Not Just Classrooms

Trilateral Consulting MEP Engineers was founded with a clear purpose — to fill the gap between paper design and real-world execution. Having spent years on sites across Goa and Maharashtra, our founders Rohan and Gopal Parab saw first-hand how poor MEP coordination created delays, cost overruns, and safety risks.

We built Trilateral to do it differently. Every design we produce is co-ordinated with architectural and structural drawings, every system we specify is chosen with long-term performance in mind, and every site we supervise is treated as if it were our own building.

Today, we work across luxury residential villas, large apartment complexes, hotel developments, hospitals, government institutional projects, and industrial facilities — bringing the same rigour and attention to every engagement regardless of scale.
